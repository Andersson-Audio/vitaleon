Vitaleon Factory.vitalbank holds the 32 factory sounds; the plugin starts on this bank and loads its first sound.
Fizmatic.vitalbank holds 54 sounds, each led by one of the 54 wavetables in Wavetables/Fizmatic.
QUB1T - Volume 1.vitalbank (95 sounds) and QUB1T - Volume 2.vitalbank (100 sounds) hold sounds by qub1t.
HOME steps through the banks with the two arrows beside the bank name and loads the first sound of the bank; PRESET lists the sounds of the bank that is shown.
Your own .vitalbank or ZIP banks: open them in LIBRARY from a USB stick or SD card and tick INSTALL.
